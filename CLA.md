## Why this file exists, in plain words

This section is a summary. It is **not** part of the agreement, and where it
and the operative text below disagree, the operative text is what binds.

omul is dual-licensed: [AGPL-3.0-only](LICENSE-AGPL-3.0) for everyone, or a
separate commercial agreement ([LICENSE-COMMERCIAL](LICENSE-COMMERCIAL)). Only
the holder of the rights in a line of code can offer that second half over it.
Without an agreement from you, your contribution reaches this project under the
AGPL alone, the commercial half can never cover it, and there is no fix after
the fact short of asking you individually or deleting the code.

So this document asks you for the rights the dual license needs, and no more:

- **You keep your copyright.** Nothing here is an assignment. You do not lose

- **The Owner promises back that your contribution stays free software.** It

  offered *beside* that, never instead of it.
- **You are not promising to maintain anything**, you give no warranty (§8),

If you cannot make the representations in §6 — most commonly because your

## How to sign

**Open the pull request first.** A bot checks whether every author of the
commits in it has signed, and comments with the exact sentence to reply with if
someone has not. Replying with that sentence, from Your own GitHub account, is
the signature — there is no form to print, and nothing to email.

The sentence is:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

You sign once. Every later pull request from the same account is covered, until
a new version of this agreement is published (§10).

**If You are signing for an entity** (§7), say so in the same comment: give the
entity's legal name and Your authority to bind it. **If GitHub is not a route
You can use**, or You are sending an employer's waiver, write to
`support@hyhyve.com` with `omul CLA` in the subject line instead. It is the same
monitored mailbox that answers licensing, trademark and security mail.

### What is recorded, and where

Your signature is stored as a public record in this repository: Your GitHub
username and account id, the pull request and comment it came from, and the
timestamp. That record is what a future licensee relies on, so it is kept for
as long as the Project exists and is not deleted on request — it is the
evidence that the grant in §2 was made. No other personal data is collected by
the signing process, and nothing is passed to a third party beyond GitHub,
which is already hosting the repository You are contributing to.

For anything else about this document, write to `support@hyhyve.com`.
