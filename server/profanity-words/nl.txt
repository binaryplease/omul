# Matching is whole-word and ignores case and accents. Dutch builds its
# harshest curses by compounding a disease onto a noun, and a whole-word list
# catches only the compounds it names: each one in common use is listed on its
# own line.
#
# What belongs here: words whose ordinary use is obscene, a slur, or a strong
# personal insult — including the English swearwords Dutch speakers use as
# loanwords. What does not: mild words (debiel, idioot), and any word whose
# everyday meaning is innocent — the bare disease names "kanker", "tyfus" and
# "tering" (a talk about cancer research is not profane), "eikel" (an acorn),
# "pik" and "mongool" (a Mongol).
