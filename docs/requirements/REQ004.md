## Description

A deck can be published as a template within its workspace and instantiated by other members. The instance is an independent deck; later edits to the template do not reach it.

## Updates

- 2026-09-04 — Shipped: a workspace publishes one of its own decks as a template its members start from. New `workspaceTemplates` collection (`StoredWorkspaceTemplateSchema`, unique per (workspace, source deck)) owned by `server/services/workspace-templates.ts`; `WorkspaceTemplateSchema` extends `DeckTemplateSchema` so one gallery and one `filterDeckTemplates` serve the built-in catalog and a workspace's own. Three routes on the workspace surface — `GET/POST /api/workspaces/:id/templates` and `DELETE …/:templateId` — plus `workspaceTemplateId` on `POST /api/presentations`. Authorization is server-side on every request against the caller's role: publishing and unpublishing are the new `canPublishWorkspaceTemplates` predicate (admin/owner), reading is `canReadWorkspace`, instantiating is `canCreateWorkspaceDecks`; a workspace the caller is not in answers the same 403 as one that does not exist, and the entry is resolved only after that check, so no route is an existence oracle. Independence is `withFreshSlideIds` applied twice — once at publish (a snapshot, so editing the deck does not reach the entry) and once at instantiation (so later edits to the template do not reach the deck) — with `copyTemplateSlides` widened to the one implementation for both kinds of template. Publishing is idempotent per deck (republish refreshes); deleting a workspace sweeps its gallery and no deck. Client: shared `components/TemplateCard.tsx` (+ category labels, guarded in `mise run check`) composed by TemplatesPage and WorkspacePage, `PublishTemplateDialog` on the deck's own card, publish/unpublish/use controls drawn disabled with their reason. Tests: `server/workspace-templates.integration.test.ts` (13) drives publishing, listing, instantiation by a second member, both refusals — non-member and ordinary member — and both directions of independence over HTTP; `server/workspace-templates.schemas.test.ts` (17) covers the vocabulary. Docs: api.md gains the routes, the role-table row and a 'Templates a workspace publishes' section; the stale 'REQ004 needs a workspace' notes in `server/templates.ts` and api.md are gone.
