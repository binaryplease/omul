## Description

As an organizer setting up a quiz question, I want the line under its reveal setting to say what the room will actually see, so that I do not expect live bars on the shared screen while the question is being answered.

The reveal group in a slide's settings carries one consequence line for the mode in force (REQ102, REQ155). For "Instant", it said "results appear as answers arrive", prefixed with "Deck default —" when the slide follows the deck. A quiz question's tally is withheld from the room until the question is over (REQ214, REQ215), so on a quiz slide that line described something the room never sees.

- **A quiz question on Instant** reads "Results appear once the question is over (time up or submissions closed).", or "Deck default — results appear once the question is over (time up or submissions closed)." when it follows the deck.
- **Unchanged:** the wording for every other slide type, and for a quiz on "On click" or "Private"; the reveal picker, its options and the deck-level picker in the presentation settings.

## Notes

Defect, **internal**: Re-Test A, N3, still present in Re-Test D (section 4) on every quiz slide set to "Deck (Instant)", in an existing deck and a new one.

- **Tests:** `src/components/SlideEditor.test.ts`, "the reveal's one line is about the room", pins the quiz wording inherited and authored, and that a quiz on the other modes and non-quiz slides on Instant keep theirs. The quiz case fails on the tree before the change.
