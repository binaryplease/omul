## Description

As an organizer who edits a deck while the audience stays connected — after a reset, before going live, or mid-session — I want every phone and every other presenter screen of the deck to apply the slides I saved without a reload, so that what they show and how they judge a question follows the deck as it now stands rather than the deck as they loaded it.

A phone judges a quiz question off its own copy of the slide: whether the participant's verdict, points and the room's results may be shown (the slide's reveal setting, REQ015–REQ017/REQ102), what the question asks and offers, which answers are accepted, how long it runs (REQ057), and which slide is at which position. Without this, a question switched to "On click" after a reset was still read as `inherit` → `instant` on a phone that stayed connected, so the phone drew the verdict and points the moment the window closed, before the presenter's reveal; and a quiz slide moved to the front was not the one a connected phone showed when the presenter started it, so it could not be answered. This repairs REQ216's "On click still waits for the presenter's reveal" for connected phones, the reveal settings of REQ015–REQ017 and REQ102, the answer window of REQ057, and the audience's copy of the deck that REQ056 and REQ090 project.

- **On save** (`PATCH /api/presentations/:id` naming `slides`, and the deck-wide reveal-mode operation of REQ018, which clears the per-slide overrides), the room is sent `presentation.slides` with the whole deck's slides.
- **What travels is the audience's copy**, projected exactly as the join fetch projects it (`withAudienceSlides`): no answer key or accepted answers while a question runs (REQ056), no presenter notes (REQ090), and nothing from the deck but its slides — never `creatorTokenHash` or `creatorId`. A room is broadcast to by presentation and a socket's claimed role proves nothing, so every client reads this frame.
- **A participant** replaces the slides it holds with the frame's, whole; runtime fields (active index, status, reveal set, opening stamps) are left to their own frames.
- **A presenter screen** does not take the frame's slides — they have no notes and, while a question runs, no key — and re-reads its own credentialed copy instead, slides only.
- **Out of scope:** a live change of the deck's audience language still takes a reload.

## Notes

Hardening, **internal**: found by a re-test of the quiz fix chain. A participant who stayed connected through a reset, a switch of a quiz question to "On click" and a save saw "Correct!" with its points before the reveal; a reload fixed it. The same mechanism left the slide order stale, so a connected phone stayed on the old first slide while the screen and freshly loaded phones ran the moved quiz question. The cause was that a save broadcast nothing about the slides, and `slide.changed`, which does carry a projected slide, was reduced to its index on the client.

As shipped (2026-10-31):

- **Server:** `broadcastDeckSlides` in `server/services/presentations.ts`, called by `updatePresentation` when the PATCH names `slides` (before the settings frames, so a phone re-reading the deck off one of those asks after it holds the saved slides) and by `setDeckResultsVisibility`.
- **Client:** `applyDeckSlides` in `src/store/session.ts` replaces the slides; `refreshPresentationSlides` re-reads `GET /api/presentations/:id` for a presenter screen. `useSessionSocket` routes the frame by the page's role: `participant` applies, `presenter` re-reads. A save bumps a revision that discards a deck re-read (`refreshDeckSlides`, REQ053/REQ018) asked before it, and the presenter's re-read discards an earlier one that lands later.
- **Not changed:** `slide.changed` is still reduced to its index. Applying its slide on a presenter screen would replace the editor's copy with the audience's; the save frame is what carries edits, including those not followed by navigation.
- **Tests:** `server/ws.integration.test.ts` (the frame on save, its projection equal to the join fetch's and free of the key, notes and owner fields, the order, typed accepted answers, the deck-wide reveal mode, no frame for a PATCH without slides); `src/store/session.test.ts` (`applyDeckSlides`, `refreshPresentationSlides`, the re-read race); `src/components/ParticipantSlideView.test.ts` (a connected phone after reset → "On click" → save → start shows no result before the reveal; a moved slide is the one shown when it starts; a saved time limit is the one counted down). All fail on the tree before this change.
