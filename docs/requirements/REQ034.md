## Description

The items a ranking slide orders can be added, removed and renamed, bounded by a per-slide item limit.

## Updates

- 2026-08-04 — Organizers define a ranking slide's items: new Slide.rankingItems[] (RankingItemSchema, defaulted to [] per ADR-0029) edited in SlideEditor's "Items" section, mirroring the scale-statements editor — add/remove rows, floor of two (one item is not an ordering), and CreatePage rejects a save with a blank item. Item cap chosen as RANKING_ITEM_LIMIT = 10, not the 5 scaleStatements allows: a scale statement is answered independently so five is a comfortable page, whereas a ranking is one ordering the participant holds in their head and physically reorders on a phone — the ceiling is what stays workable, and past ten the list needs scrolling while being moved through. Ten also keeps an encoded full ordering of UUID-ided items inside VoteSchema.value's 500-character budget (pinned by a test). The number is declared once in server/schemas.ts and composed by both the schema's .max() and the editor's add-item button, which disables with "Max 10 items" rather than disappearing (ADR-0025/ADR-0026). Partial orderings are accepted per this requirement's note — the Borda tally scores only what a ballot placed and reports rankedCount/notRanked per item.
