## Description

A quiz slide where the participant picks one option, holds exactly one final answer for the session, and is scored on correctness and speed.

## Updates

- 2026-08-06 — Quiz competition select-answer shipped: participants pick one option on a quiz slide under a live countdown, and the answer is final (a second submission is refused with 400) because speed is part of the score. Points combine correctness and speed — QUIZ_CORRECT_POINTS (500) for a correct answer however late, plus up to QUIZ_SPEED_POINTS (500) falling linearly to zero at the deadline; an untimed question awards the speed half in full so every question is worth the same at its best. maxSelectionsFor() now returns 1 for a quiz on every surface, so a hand-built multi-select quiz cannot reach the scoring boundary. Participant surface disables the cards with a stated reason once answered or closed (ADR-0025) and shows the participant's own verdict and points after the question. Server tests in server/quiz.integration.test.ts + server/quiz.schemas.test.ts.
- 2026-08-07 — Defect found in review and fixed under REQ147: the one-answer rule was a read-modify-write with an awaited lookup in the middle, so two submissions from one participant arriving at once both persisted — a double-tap or a retried POST left the deck holding two standing answers permanently, since a quiz is the one slide type that refuses the later submission that would have collapsed them. The quiz branch of submitVote now runs its check-and-insert through a keyed queue (server/keyed-lock.ts) keyed per participant per question, and the loser of a race reads the winner's row and is refused with the quiz-already-answered reason it was always meant to meet.
