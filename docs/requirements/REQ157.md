## Description

In the authoring surfaces, structural and explanatory text — section headings, field labels' hints, inline notes, and text-bearing controls such as row-remove buttons and "Add …" links — meets WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1) in both colour schemes. Concretely: nothing structural sits on `--color-text-dim`, and accent-coloured text resolves through a per-scheme accent-for-text token rather than the raw accent.

## Notes

Measured against the tokens in `src/index.css` (WCAG 2.x relative luminance, against `--color-surface` per scheme):

- `--color-text-dim` on the dark surface: **2.63:1**; on the light surface: **4.34:1** — both below AA for small text, and this token carries the editor's section headings, every field hint, and the row-remove controls today.
- `--color-accent` (#ff6b35) as text on the light surface: **2.59:1** — this is the "Add option" / "Add item" / "Add field" links.
- `--color-text-muted` passes in both schemes (5.29:1 dark, 6.92:1 light) and is the correct floor for explanatory text.

The fix is a token pass, not a redesign: move structural text in the shared primitives (`Section`, `Field`, `ColorField` in `src/components/EditorControls.tsx`, and the equivalent classes in the slide editor) from `text-text-dim` to `text-text-muted` or stronger, and add a `--color-accent-text` token that is the accent on dark and a darkened accent (AA-passing, e.g. #c2410c) on light, used wherever the accent is text rather than a surface.

Scope: the authoring surfaces (slide editor, deck settings). Other uses of `text-dim` — de-emphasis inside already-passing contexts, decorative separators — are not in scope, but any that carry load-bearing words on these surfaces are.

Independent of the canvas-first rework (REQ152–REQ156): whichever editor layout is live, these tokens are what its structure is drawn in — land it before or with the rework, not after.
