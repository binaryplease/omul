## Description

A slide carries notes rendered only on the presenter surface. They are never included in a participant payload, an export or a shared results link.

## Updates

- 2026-08-08 — Slides carry presenter-only notes: SlideSchema.notes (default "", ADR-0029), authored in the slide editor's Content panel, rendered by a shared PresenterNotes panel on the live presenter page (toggle chip, closed by default) and the preview's shared-screen pane. Withholding is on the wire: withoutPresenterNotes + withAudienceSlides compose the audience projection, applied by sanitize(), the slide.changed broadcast and the preview's participant pane, so the deck fetch without a credential, the join payload, the public list, every broadcast and the XLSX export carry notes: "". Covered by server/notes.schemas.test.ts, server/notes.integration.test.ts and src/components/PresenterNotes.test.ts; docs/api.md documents the boundary.
