## Description

A slide type where a participant distributes a fixed point budget across items; the tally sums the allocations per item. A submission whose allocations do not total the budget is refused.

## Updates

- 2026-08-05 — 100 Points slide type shipped end to end: "points" joins SlideTypeEnum and INTERACTIVE_SLIDE_TYPES; a participant's whole allocation is one vote encoded into VoteSchema.value by encodePoints/decodePoints (itemId:points pairs) so no new stored field or endpoint was needed. Exactly POINTS_BUDGET (100) is enforced at the boundary by decodePoints (ADR-0013) — an under- or over-spent ballot is rejected with 400 rather than stored and left to skew the shares. One participant holds one budget; re-submitting replaces it. getSlideResults reports points, share of everything distributed (shares sum to 100%), funderCount/notFunded, and averagePoints among funders as an explicit null when nobody funded an item (ADR-0024). Participant surface splits the budget with +/- steppers and a number field over a running remainder; controls that cannot act are disabled with an explanation (ADR-0025). Tests: server/points.schemas.test.ts + server/points.integration.test.ts. Verified in the browser: two participants allocated, the shared screen rendered the aggregated distribution live.
