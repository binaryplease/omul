## Description

On the Create/Edit page, the central stage renders the selected slide as the room will see it — through the same rendering path and deck theming the preview pane uses today — as the primary object on the page, at every viewport width. The `xl`-only preview aside is retired: no viewport size loses sight of the slide, because the canvas *is* the slide. The app chrome around it (top bar, slide rail, settings column) stays in the app's own colour scheme while the canvas wears the deck's theme, so the boundary between "our editor" and "the room's screen" is visible rather than explained.

## Notes

The canvas is `SlidePreview` promoted, not a new renderer: today `src/pages/CreatePage.tsx` renders `<SlidePreview>` inside `<DeckThemeScope>` in a `hidden xl:flex` 384 px aside (REQ079 is why the pane is themed while the chrome is not). That one rendering path moves to the stage centre and becomes the editing surface; the aside is deleted. One rendering path before, one after — the editor can never show a slide the room would not get (the ADR-0026 discipline).

Why this is a defect worth a requirement: at 1600×1000 the per-slide form measures 2 653–3 183 px of scroll depending on slide type, while the slide itself renders at 384 px wide — and not at all below 1280 px viewport width. The thing being authored is the least visible object on the page that authors it.

A caption on the canvas frame names what the surface is (this is the room's screen), so the theming boundary is stated where it is seen.

Out of scope, arriving as their own requirements: in-place editing of the question and options (REQ153), the results view of the canvas (REQ154), the compacted settings column (REQ155), the notes strip (REQ156). Out of scope entirely for this slice: behaviour badges pinned to the canvas (reveal mode, countdown, defect flags on the slide edge) — a later iteration, deliberately deferred pending design refinement.

## Updates

- 2026-08-13 — Editor stage is now the slide: SlidePreview promoted to the page centre via <SlideCanvas/> (caption + DeckThemeScope frame) at every viewport width, the per-slide form and deck settings moved to the column beside it, and the hidden xl:flex 384px preview aside deleted — with a convention guard pinning the single SlidePreview render site.
