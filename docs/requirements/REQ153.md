## Description

The slide's question (or heading/caption, per type) is edited directly on the canvas (REQ152), at the size, colour and position the room will see it — an authored text size or colour is larger or recoloured text under the author's cursor, not a control's claim about it. On choice-shaped types, the option rows are likewise edited inside the option cards on the canvas, with add, remove and mark-correct affordances attached to the rows they act on.

Editing affordances — hover outlines, the add-option row, the floating correct/remove tools — are an editor-only layer that no participant, presenter or shared-results surface ever renders.

All mutations flow through the existing editor-store actions (`updateSlide`, `updateOption`, `addOption`, `removeOption` in `src/store/editor.ts`): the CRDT-ready seam is unchanged, and this surface stays presentational.

Slide types whose answer shape is a structured form rather than a list of visible rows — form fields, grid items and axes, scale statements and labels, accepted answers of a typed quiz, the guess-number range, the pin target — keep those field groups in the settings column (REQ155). Their question still edits in place on the canvas.

## Notes

The authoring contracts this surface carries today move with it unchanged in substance: option management (REQ012), marking correct answers (REQ013), and every save-time validation in `CreatePage.handleSubmit` (blank options, missing URLs, unreachable references) keep exactly their current rules — this requirement relocates where the fields live, not what they accept.

Implementation constraints:

- An empty question shows the type's placeholder on the canvas, in the muted deck-theme colour, so an untitled slide is visibly untitled rather than blank.
- Typing must not rebuild the canvas per keystroke — the caret has to survive; patch the dependent surfaces (rail thumbnail text) directly and re-render structurally only on add/remove/reorder.
- Canvas fields need real accessibility: `aria-label`s naming the field, standard focus treatment, and full keyboard reachability of the add/remove/mark-correct tools (they may reveal on hover, but must also reveal on focus-within).
- The mark-correct affordance's resting state must be visible without hover — a marked-correct option shows its check at rest, since the answer key is part of what the author needs to see at a glance.

Out of scope for this slice: behaviour badges on the canvas (deferred iteration); the results view (REQ154).
