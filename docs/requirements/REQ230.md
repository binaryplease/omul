## Description

As an organizer who pages through a deck before going live while phones and a second presenter screen are already connected, I want every one of those screens to be on the slide the presentation starts on the moment I start it, so that the room never sees a slide I have not reached — above all a quiz question, with "Start timer" on the second screen, ahead of its time.

Navigating a draft moves the deck's active slide and tells the room (`slide.changed`), so a connected screen follows it. Going live puts the deck back on the first slide, but the room was told only that the deck was live, its session clock and its opening stamps (REQ108, REQ227) — not where it now stood. A phone or second screen that followed the draft therefore stayed on the slide last picked there, while the presenter who clicked and the server were on the first. This repairs REQ226's "on screen, closed to answers, until the presenter starts its timer" and REQ227's "going live must leave no client holding" draft state, both of which assumed connected screens were on the slide the deck went live on.

- **The go-live frame** (`presentation.started`) names the slide the deck went live on with REQ208's `slideIndex` and `slideId`.
- **Every client** — participant and presenter screen alike — takes `slideIndex` in place of the index it holds. A frame without it (an older server) leaves the index held.
- **Unchanged:** the presenter screen that clicked already set its index off its own start response; slide navigation after going live is `slide.changed`'s, as before.

## Notes

Defect, **internal**: found by a re-test of the quiz fix chain. A phone and a second presenter screen connected while the presenter selected the second slide of a draft stayed on it after go-live; the presenter and freshly loaded screens were on the first. The defect predates the quiz work.

As shipped (2026-10-31):

- **Server:** `startPresentation` in `server/services/presentations.ts` adds `activeSlideIndex` (read off the updated deck) to the `presentation.started` broadcast.
- **Client:** `applyStarted` in `src/store/session.ts` patches `activeSlideIndex` when the frame carries a number; `useSessionSocket` already routes the frame to it for both roles.
- **`cli/`:** not touched — the command-line client reads no WebSocket frames, and `cli/protocol.ts` restates nothing of this one.
- **Tests:** `server/ws.integration.test.ts` (the frame's shape equals the start response's index; a participant that followed draft navigation to the second slide receives `activeSlideIndex: 0`); `src/store/session.test.ts` (`applyStarted` moves a screen on the second slide to the frame's index; a frame without one leaves the index held); `src/components/ParticipantSlideView.test.ts` (a phone that followed the draft to a quiz question shows the first slide after go-live, not the question). All but the older-server guard fail on the tree before this change.

As changed (2026-11-25): the frame's `activeSlideIndex` named the same slide as REQ208's `slideIndex`, and `applyStarted` already moved the index on `slideIndex`. The duplicate field is gone from the broadcast, from `applyStarted`'s payload type and handler, and from the `presentation.started` row in `docs/api.md`. The tests above assert the same behaviour through `slideIndex` / `slideId`; the store's older-server guard is the existing "a start frame without a slide leaves the slide this screen holds" test.
