## Description

The items a 100-points slide allocates across can be added, removed and renamed, bounded by a per-slide item limit.

## Updates

- 2026-08-05 — New Slide.pointsItems[], defaulted to [] (ADR-0029), edited in a new "Items" section in the editor's Content panel (ADR-0031) mirroring the ranking-items and scale-statements editors; the editor seeds two blank rows and CreatePage rejects a save with fewer than two items or a blank one. POINTS_ITEM_LIMIT = 8 — declared once and composed by both the schema's .max() and the editor's add-item button (ADR-0026). Eight rather than the ten rankingItems allow: ranking and grid items are answered independently, while 100 Points items share one budget, so every change forces the participant to re-check the remainder against all of them at once — and eight keeps an encoded allocation at ~330 of value's 500 characters with UUID ids, where the hard ceiling is twelve. Items appear in the slide preview and the rail thumbnail; POINTS_BUDGET is the format, not an authored field, so there is no editor control for it.
