## Description

As an organizer running a scored quiz, I want a participant's own verdict on a quiz question — whether they were right, what it scored, and every total or place that counts it — to stay off the wire to everybody who cannot edit the deck until the audience may see that question's solution, so that nobody can learn the answer key by reading their own result. A verdict is the key read one answer at a time: a participant id is minted by the browser, so anybody can answer under a fresh id per option and read each verdict back, and the leaderboard sent to every phone after each answer names the same thing through a row's points. This repairs REQ056 (quiz scoring and the participant's scorecard) and REQ059 (the leaderboard), and completes REQ215, which withheld the room's tally on the same terms but left the per-participant routes open.

"May see the solution" is two gates together: the question is over (its window has run out with its grace, it was closed after opening, it was revealed, or the deck has ended), **and** its reveal setting lets the room see its results (`instant` always, `on-click` once the presenter reveals that slide, `private` never — not even after the deck has ended). Under that gate:

- **Scorecard** (`GET /api/presentations/:id/scorecard`): the question's entry reads `isCorrect` and `points` as an explicit `null`, and the question counts towards none of `correctCount`, `totalPoints` and `rank` (nor `rankedCount`, which is read off the same board). `answered`, `optionId`, `answer` and `elapsedMs` stay.
- **Leaderboard standings** (`results.updated` of type `leaderboard` from `sendStandings`, and the public results reads of a leaderboard slide): summed only over the released questions, so a running or unrevealed question moves no row. The results link does not lift this.
- **Quiz frames** carry no `correctCount` while withheld (already true since REQ215; pinned again beside the standings).
- **The deck the audience is sent** (`GET /api/join/:code`, the public `GET /api/presentations/:id`, `slide.changed`, `presentation.slides`): a quiz question's `isCorrect` marks and accepted answers stay off it on the same gate, because the key beside a participant's own answer is their verdict.
- **When it is released**, the phone shows the verdict and points without a reload, and the standings count the question from then on.

## Notes

Hardening, **internal**: found in the cumulative review of the quiz fix chain (scorecard leak) and confirmed by a re-test over the wire, which also showed the leaderboard frame carrying a right answer's points under "On click" while the quiz frame beside it was already withheld.

As shipped (2026-10-31):

- **The gate** is `quizVerdictVisibleToAudience` in `server/schemas.ts`: `solutionVisibleToAudience && tallyVisibleToAudience` on a quiz slide, `true` on any other type. `quizVerdictVisibleToCaller` adds the editor exemption (`canEdit` only — like REQ215, the results link does not lift it, because a verdict read early is a running question's key).
- **The scorecard** is always the audience's reading, whoever asks: its only credential is the browser-minted participant id. A withheld entry is read off `points === null` (`scorecardVerdictWithheld`), since a released entry always carries a number there, `0` when unanswered. `rank` and `rankedCount` come from a board ranked over the released questions only, so the card's place agrees with the board the room is sent.
- **The standings** are built by `leaderboardFrom(presentationId, deck, counted, names)`: ranked over `counted` (`quizDeckCountedFor`, the questions this caller may read verdicts of), while `quizCount` and `maxPoints` still describe the whole deck so the board's "top 5 across 4 questions" does not shift as questions are released. Someone who has answered only withheld questions is not ranked yet.
- **Re-broadcast when a question is released.** The quiz frame's re-send at window close or close-to-submissions already carried the standings with it. The board is now also re-sent on a reveal or un-reveal (`setSlideRevealed`), on ending the deck, and on a deck-wide reveal-mode change; the editor's PATCH already re-sent it.
- **The participant's screen** (`ParticipantSlideView`) draws no verdict for a withheld entry (`ownQuizResultFor`), where it would otherwise have read the `null` as "Not quite". It re-reads the card when the question ends (`quizOver`) and when the question's tally comes out (`quizTallyPublished`) — the latter covers a card fetched on the phone's own clock that reached the server a moment before the window closed.
- **Behaviour changes beyond the leak**, both following from the gate: an untimed live question's verdict now arrives when it is revealed, closed or the deck ends, rather than as soon as the participant answered; and a survey deck's quiz verdicts arrive when the presenter reveals the question or the survey ends. In both, the deck's own copy of the question already withheld the key until then.
- **Editors were unchanged here.** The presenter's credentialed read of a leaderboard slide still counted every question, so a board projected while a question was unrevealed or private showed points the phones' copy did not yet count. Whether the projected board should follow the audience's reading was left open here. REQ233 decided that it does: the board the presenting view reads now counts only released questions, and an editor reads the whole standings only on the organizer's own surfaces.
- **Tests:** `server/quiz-verdict-withheld.integration.test.ts` (fails on the tree before this change). The scoring and leaderboard suites now read their scorecards and boards after revealing the deck's questions, since they test arithmetic, not release; the WebSocket leaderboard tests reveal the question before answering for the same reason.

## Updates

- 2026-10-31 — Filed and shipped. The participant scorecard withholds isCorrect and points (explicit null) and leaves the slide out of correctCount, totalPoints and rank while a quiz question is running or its reveal setting keeps its results from the room (on-click until revealed, private always); the leaderboard standings sent to non-editors and the results link count only released questions; quiz frames carry no correctCount. Gate quizVerdictVisibleToAudience (solutionVisibleToAudience and tallyVisibleToAudience), editor-only exemption. Standings re-broadcast on reveal, end and reveal-mode change. The participant view draws nothing for a withheld entry and re-reads the card when the question ends or its tally is published. Scoring, leaderboard and WebSocket suites now read after revealing.
- 2026-10-31 — Fix from the chain's security review: the deck payload still carried a quiz question's answer key once its window closed, whatever its reveal setting, so under 'On click' before the reveal and under 'Private' always a participant could read their own verdict off the join payload beside the withheld scorecard. withAudienceSolutions now holds a quiz's isCorrect marks and accepted answers on quizVerdictVisibleToAudience (over and its reveal setting allows) rather than on the question being over alone; every other slide type is unchanged. Tests in server/quiz-verdict-withheld.integration.test.ts check the join payload, the public deck read and the presentation.slides frame under 'Private' (also after the deck ends) and 'On click' (until the reveal), select and typed; three of them fail on the tree before this fix.
- 2026-11-01 — The open design question in Notes (whether the projected board follows the audience's reading) is decided and shipped as REQ233: the leaderboard the presenting view reads with the edit token now counts only released questions, and an editor reads the whole standings only on the results page, its breakdown, the export and the preview. The Notes line is corrected to point there.
