## Description

As a presenter who made presentations without an account, I want the home page to tell me they live only in this browser and to offer me an account, so that clearing the browser's data does not lose them for good.

## Notes

**What happens today.** A presentation created without an account is held by one thing: its edit token, in this browser's `localStorage` under `TOKEN_KEY` (`src/storage.ts`). The server stores only the token's hash, so a browser whose data is cleared loses every such deck with no way back. The way out already exists — email sign-up and sign-in through Better Auth (`src/auth.tsx`), after which `claimLocalPresentations()` in `src/api.ts`, called from `src/pages/HomePage.tsx`, attaches every locally held deck to the account — but nothing on the home page says so.

**Desired outcome.** On the home page, a browser that is not signed in and holds at least one presentation sees a notice saying the presentations are stored only in this browser, with a control that opens the existing sign-in dialog on its "Create account" tab. Signing up or signing in removes the notice for good: a browser that has been signed in to an account is never shown it again, including after signing out or after its session expires, and the header's Sign in button is its way back. (Neither the claim nor signing out removes the local edit tokens, so a signed-out browser still lists its claimed decks; telling it they are "saved only in this browser" and opening "Create account" for an address that already has an account would be false.) The notice is an offer, never a gate: it can be closed, the close is remembered in this browser, and nothing on the page is blocked while it is shown.

**Done when.**

- A browser that has never been signed in and holds one or more presentations sees the notice above "Your Presentations"; one with none, a signed-in one, or a signed-out one that has been signed in before, does not, and it is not drawn while the session or the list is still loading.
- The notice's action opens the sign-in dialog on "Create account"; after signing up or signing in, the notice is gone and the decks are owned by the account, and it stays gone after signing out.
- Closing the notice keeps it closed across reloads of this browser.
- The notice's words come from `src/i18n.ts` and exist in every language the dictionary carries; the home page draws them in English, like the rest of its chrome.
- The notice composes the shared primitives in `docs/frontend.md` (lucide icons, `ICON_BUTTON_HOVER`), and its close control carries an accessible name.

**Out of scope.** A passwordless recovery-email mechanism or any new server endpoint; reminders anywhere but the home page; a redesign of the sign-in dialog.
