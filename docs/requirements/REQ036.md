## Description

Q&A is switched on or off for a presentation as a whole rather than per slide, and the setting is broadcast to joined participants.

## Updates

- 2026-08-06 — Q&A shipped as a presentation-wide interactivity layer, not a slide type: qaEnabled on the presentation schema (default off), POST /presentations/:id/qa/settings behind owner-or-edit-token, POST /presentations/:id/qa to ask from whatever slide is on screen, and a qaQuestions collection keyed by presentationId. Asking meets the same submission rule a vote does (live deck started, survey deck not ended); a reset clears the questions with the votes. Presenter gets a queue panel beside the slide with the layer's switch on it; participants get a composer under every slide when it is on. Covered by server/qa.schemas.test.ts + server/qa.integration.test.ts; docs/api.md, architecture.md and frontend.md updated.
- 2026-08-07 — Review cleanup (ADR-0025): the presenter screen's Q&A queue button was wrapped in an isOwner guard, so a viewer who does not hold the deck found nothing there. It is now rendered always and disabled with its reason in aria-label/title, matching the export and reset chips beside it. The count is withheld from a non-owner rather than shown as 0 — their own GET /qa answers with their own questions rather than the room's (REQ037), so the chip reads an explicit em-dash (ADR-0024) and keeps its width, leaving the row free of layout shift. No behaviour change for the owner.
