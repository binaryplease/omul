## Description

A pin slide carries the image participants pin on, supplied as a URL and rendered by the browser — .png, .gif, .jpg, .jpeg, .svg, .webp, .avif, .heic and .heif all work. No extension allowlist is applied: what is displayable is the browser's answer to give, and an allowlist would refuse pictures that work while still admitting URLs that are not images. Coordinates are stored per mille of the image, so every participant pins into the same space whatever their viewport.

## Updates

- 2026-08-06 — The organizer supplies the interaction area as the slide's existing mediaUrl/mediaAlt (repo media-by-URL convention, no upload subsystem), authored in the editor's Content panel with the format guidance; pinImageFor() is the single read and slideMediaIsInteractionArea() keeps a pin slide's canvas from also being drawn as an illustration on participant, presenter, preview and thumbnail surfaces. A slide with no image accepts no pins at the boundary and is refused at save.
