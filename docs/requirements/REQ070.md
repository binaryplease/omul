## Description

A slide carries an optional background color overriding the theme's, without changing the theme itself.

## Updates

- 2026-08-11 — A slide carries backgroundColor (#rgb/#rrggbb, empty means the theme's), painted by SlideBackground under the deck's wash and over nothing else. What the canvas implies is derived rather than authored: the raised surfaces, the borders and — when the slide named no text colour — the words come from neutralRampTokens(), the very function the deck's own brand derivation uses, so a light slide in a dark deck cannot end up with near-white words on near-white paper. The deck's theme is untouched: it is a pure function of the deck, and the next slide that authored nothing still wears it.
- 2026-08-11 — Review fix (NST8763, finding 1): the surfaces an authored canvas implies now come from surfaceRampTokens() and the words from textRampTokens() — neutralRampTokens() was split in two, so the entry above naming it is stale. Behaviour for an authored canvas is unchanged apart from the derived text steps now being held to a contrast floor (see REQ019).
