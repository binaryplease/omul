## Description

As a deck author, I want to click the question, a text block or the slide's background on the editor canvas and get the controls that change exactly that element, so that I restyle what I am looking at instead of scrolling the settings column to find the right group.

Selecting the question or another text element on the canvas (REQ152, REQ153) shows, next to it, the style controls that apply to it: its colour (REQ019) and text size (REQ091), and the typeface where the slide can carry one (REQ092). Selecting the slide's empty background shows the background's controls: its colour (REQ070) and its picture (REQ071). Each control writes through the same editor-store actions as its counterpart in the settings column, so both stay in step, and the settings column keeps every control it has now for keyboard and screen-reader users.

**Done when.** On a choice slide, a text slide and an image slide, clicking the question, then a text element, then the background brings up that element's style controls without the settings column scrolling. A change made there shows on the canvas at once and is reflected in the "Style" group, and the other way round. Escape or a click elsewhere closes the controls. The controls are reachable by keyboard from the selected element. No participant, presenter or shared-results surface renders any of it. `mise run check` and `mise run test`, as `AGENTS.md` declares them, apply unchanged.

## Notes

Today every look-related control sits in the "Style" group, `StyleGroup` in `src/components/SlideEditor.tsx` (around line 2500), rendered last in the settings column after the content, answers, scoring and results groups (around line 850). On a choice slide the author scrolls past all of them to reach a colour. The background picture is a URL field inside that group.

Constraints carried over from REQ153: selection affordances are an editor-only layer; typing on the canvas must not rebuild it per keystroke; controls reveal on focus as well as on hover.

A presenter reply in the same feedback also asked for "more contrast" in designing a presentation. Whether that means the editor's own interface or the decks it produces is not settled, so it is not part of this requirement.
