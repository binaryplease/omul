## Description

A text passage can carry a language distinct from the deck's, emitted as a lang attribute so a screen reader switches pronunciation.

## Notes

The syntax is a Pandoc-style bracketed span, `[passage]{lang=fr}`, parsed in
`src/components/SlideText.tsx` (`parseSlideInline`, so every surface that
composes `<SlideText/>` gets it) and rendered as a `<span>` carrying `lang` and
nothing else. The passage closes on the bracket that balances its opener, so
markup inside it — a link included — still renders. The tag rule is
`isSlideLanguageTag`: BCP 47's shape without its registry — two or three
letters, then hyphen-separated subtags of one to eight letters or digits, 35
characters at most. A span whose tag fails, or whose braces hold anything but
`lang=<tag>`, is shown exactly as typed. The PDF export's
`plainTextFromMarkdown` (`server/deck-pdf.ts`) restates the rule, keeps the
passage's words and drops the marking; `SlideText.test.ts` holds the two
copies together. Authored text stays verbatim on the server: no schema change.
