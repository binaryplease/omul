## Description

As a participant using a screen reader, I want to hear that my answer went through and is locked in, how it scored, why an option no longer takes a tap and how much time is left, and I want keyboard focus to stay where I was when I answer, so that a quiz question is something I can take part in rather than a screen that falls silent. This repairs REQ054, REQ055 and REQ057 (the quiz question, its typed form and its countdown) and REQ056 (the verdict a participant is shown), which drew all of that for the eye only.

- The answer controls are a group named by the question (`aria-labelledby` on the participation fieldset, pointing at the slide's heading).
- Under the controls sits a polite live region, mounted whether or not it has anything to say: the lock reason ("Your answer is locked in", "The time for this question is over"), "Sending…" while an answer is in flight, and, once the question is over, the verdict and its points. It does not repeat itself (`aria-atomic="false"`), so the verdict arriving reads as "Correct! +836 points".
- The toasts ("Vote submitted!", a refusal's reason) are a polite live region on every screen.
- The countdown's number is a `timer`, which is silent. Beside it a polite region speaks at thresholds only: "30 seconds left" and "10 seconds left", each only when the window is longer than that, and "Time's up". A threshold's words are in the deck's language (`quizSecondsLeftSpoken`).
- A locked or busy option, the typed answer field and its submit button say they are unavailable with `aria-disabled` (the field is also `readOnly`) instead of `disabled`, and their clicks are guarded. The control the participant just used keeps focus instead of dropping it to `<body>`.

## Notes

Defect, **internal**: found by a UI test pass of the quiz feature on `f3bd62e` (major M5: WCAG 4.1.3, 2.4.3, 4.1.2). The participant page had no `aria-live`, `role=status`, `role=alert` or `role=timer` region, the answer group had no name, and submitting disabled the focused button.

**What closes it.**

- `src/components/QuizTimer.tsx`: `quizCountdownAnnouncement` (pure: the last threshold crossed, `"expired"`, or `null`), `QUIZ_TIMER_SPOKEN_THRESHOLDS`, and the `timer` and `status` elements in `QuizTimer`. The countdown now carries its `timeLimit`, so the thresholds need no extra prop. The presenter screen wears the same wrapper, with English words.
- `src/components/ParticipantSlideView.tsx`: `AnswerStatus` (the live region, with the verdict as its children), `choiceCardState` (one reading of a card's selected / pending / unavailable / dimmed state, shared by its class, its ARIA state and its click guard), and the heading id passed to `ParticipationGate`.
- `src/components/LiveRoom.tsx`: `ParticipationGate` takes `labelledBy`.
- `src/components/ui/Toast.tsx`: the toast stack is `role="status"`.
- `src/index.css`: `.btn-primary` and `.input` draw `[aria-disabled="true"]` the same as `:disabled`, so one rule owns the look of both.

**Deliberately not here.** A slide the presenter closes to submissions (REQ111) still disables its controls through the fieldset's `disabled`. That is a different event, and a fieldset cannot make its descendants `aria-disabled`.
