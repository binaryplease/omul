## Description

Slide text can carry hyperlinks, rendered clickable on the presenter and participant surfaces.

## Notes

The PDF export reduces a link to `label (address)`, since paper cannot be clicked. That reduction (`reduceLinks` in `server/deck-pdf.ts`), and the bold reduction next to it, are held to linear work in the text by REQ213; the expression they replaced was quadratic.
