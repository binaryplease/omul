## Description

Everything about a slide that is not authored on the canvas lives in one compact settings column beside it (~300 px), replacing the two stacked Content/Settings panels: slide type, answer rules, scoring and timing, the reveal override, chart style and values, and appearance (text size, colours, layout). The column is composed from the shared `EditorControls` primitives and is short enough that a common slide type (choice, quiz, word cloud) fits without scrolling.

Two invariants the column must state visibly:

1. **Departure from the default is marked.** A group whose setting differs from its deck-level or product default carries a marker at the group level and a one-gesture reset back to the default. An inherited colour reads as "Theme" — a word, not a hex that looks authored.
2. **Prose shrinks to consequence lines.** The canvas shows what a setting does (REQ152/REQ153/REQ154), so paragraphs describing outcomes are deleted; what remains is at most one line per group stating the consequence of the current choice (e.g. the reveal mode's one-sentence meaning), not descriptions of the choices not taken.

## Notes

This is the remainder of `src/components/SlideEditor.tsx` after REQ153 moves the question and option rows onto the canvas. The `EditorPanel` wrapper (Content/Settings frames) retires from this surface — it has no other consumer; `PanelHeader` stays with `DeckSettings` in `CreatePage`, which keeps its current form. Compose `Segmented`, tile-style `ChoiceCards`, and `ColorField` collapsed to swatch chips that expand to the picker + hex box on demand.

Group order follows the authoring flow: type → answers → scoring → results (reveal, then chart) → style. Slide types whose answer shape is structured (form builder, grid axes and items, scale statements, accepted quiz answers, guess range, pin target fields) keep their field groups here, under the answers group — those types may scroll; the common types must not. The pin target keeps `PinCanvas` as its spatial editor.

Postures that carry over unchanged: a control that is unavailable stays visible, disabled, and says why (e.g. the quiz countdown on an audience-paced deck); one descriptor per enumerated choice, shared with any other surface that offers it; the reveal override's semantics (REQ102 / REQ015–REQ018) and its "inherit" option showing the deck default it defers to.

The per-slide-type section ownership (which groups exist for which type) should be one declared descriptor next to the type descriptor (`SLIDE_TYPE_LABELS` in `SlideTypeMenu`), composed by the column — not re-derived by scattered conditionals — so the column and any future consumer cannot disagree about what a type owns.

Out of scope for this slice: canvas behaviour badges — reveal and countdown are authored here and only here for now, with nothing pinned to the canvas; that iteration is deferred.
