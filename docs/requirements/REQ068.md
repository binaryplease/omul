## Description

A slide can embed a Miro board that participants interact with inside the presentation flow.

## Updates

- 2026-08-11 — Shipped as part of the 'embed' content slide type. slideEmbedFor() normalises a miro.com/app/board/<id> link to miro.com/app/live-embed/<id>/ — the board with its own toolbar rather than the view-only frame, so the room works in it on the shared screen and on their phones; a share_link_id the link carries is kept, since a link-shared board is only reachable through it. What a participant may actually do in the board stays Miro's own sharing setting: the slide frames a board, it does not grant access to one. EMBED_PROVIDERS marks Miro interactive, which is what gives the board its squarer frame and the only clipboard-write grant among the three. Tests: server/embed.schemas.test.ts, server/embed.integration.test.ts.
