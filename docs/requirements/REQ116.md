## Description

The results surface can render a question broken down by another slide's answers — the read-side counterpart of REQ020's aggregation, over a whole deck rather than one slide.

## Updates

- 2026-08-14 — Read side shipped: src/components/SegmentedResults.tsx draws a 'break these results down by' picker under every slide on the /results/:id surface, across the whole deck. Entries come from the server's own segmentSourcesFor descriptor, so ineligible earlier slides are drawn disabled with the API's own sentence (ADR-0025) rather than dropped; each group is rendered by the same ResultsDisplay the unsegmented tally uses (ADR-0027), and a group too small to publish states that instead of drawing an empty panel. Verified in the browser against a seeded room, as organizer and as anonymous visitor.
- 2026-08-14 — Review NST9136 follow-up on the read side: a held-back group draws no head count (the server no longer sends one) and its sentence now covers both cases it can mean — the group too small to publish, and the group held back beside it so the first cannot be subtracted out. A wholly withheld breakdown says which reason it met, since 'the organizer kept a slide's results back' and 'this slide's results name individual people' are different news and only one of them is something the organizer can change.
