## Description

A guess slide carries a step, counted from the range's low end. A submission off the grid is refused rather than snapped to the nearest legal value.

## Updates

- 2026-08-05 — Step size shipped as guessRange.step (integer, min 1, default 1). Counted **from the range low end**, not from zero — 1-10 in twos offers 1,3,5,7,9, because the grid an organizer authors starts where their range starts — and that is enforced in one place, decodeGuess: (guess - min) % step must be 0, else 400. Off-step submissions are rejected rather than snapped to the nearest legal value, for the same reason out-of-range ones are not clamped: the number is the whole answer, so an adjusted one is an estimate the participant never made. A step of 0 or a fractional one is rejected by the schema at the boundary (422 at create), and isUsableGuessRange additionally refuses a step wider than the span, which would leave a single possible answer. guessBucketsFor() aligns the distribution columns to the step, so the histogram plots the values the slide actually offers rather than the integers between them, and groups a whole number of steps per column once a range exceeds GUESS_BUCKET_LIMIT (24) columns. Participant surface: the number input carries min/max/step, the ± buttons move by exactly one authored step (a first tap from an off-grid or empty entry lands *on* the grid), and the step is stated as "In steps of 2" above the button; a doomed submission is blocked with "That number is not one of the selectable values" rather than silently sent (ADR-0025). Editor: a Step field with a live count of selectable values. Tests: server/guess.schemas.test.ts (grid counted from min; a max off the grid is a bound, not a selectable value; columns follow the step and cover every value exactly once), server/guess.integration.test.ts (5 rejected with 400 on a step-2 slide and not snapped to 4 or 6; 6 accepted). Verified in the browser: 13 on a 0-20/step-2 slide disabled the submit button with the off-step explanation.
