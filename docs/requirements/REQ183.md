## Description

As someone who signs up, creates a presentation or joins a room, I want the imprint, the privacy policy and the terms to be reachable from the app and named to me before I commit, so that the operator meets § 312i Abs. 1 S. 1 Nr. 2 and Nr. 4 BGB and so that the published terms actually become part of the contract under § 305 Abs. 2 BGB instead of being a document nobody was ever shown.

## Notes

**What happens today.** The application ships no legal text and no link to one. `rg -li "impressum|datenschutz|agb|nutzungsbedingungen|terms of service" src server` returns nothing at `acae328`, and there is no application chrome that could carry such links — the only `footer` in the frontend is a slide-internal caption block in `src/components/SlidePreview.tsx:504`, not a page footer. The texts themselves exist and are published on the marketing site (imprint, privacy policy, terms); the product simply never points at them, at any route, at any moment.

**The three points at which a contract is concluded.** Account sign-up — the `signup` branch of the auth form in `src/auth.tsx`, whose mode toggle is at `:351` and whose submit button reads "Create account" at `:412`. Creating a presentation without an account — `api.createPresentation` at `src/pages/CreatePage.tsx:656`. Joining a room with a six-digit code — `api.joinByCode` at `src/pages/JoinPage.tsx:34`, reached from the "Join with Code" entry point at `src/pages/HomePage.tsx:248`. None of the three shows a legal text, a link to one, or a sentence naming the terms.

**What the obligation already covers and what it does not.** § 312i Abs. 1 S. 1 BGB applies to contracts concluded in electronic commerce, against consumers and businesses alike, and does not turn on the service being free of charge. Two of its four duties are met. Nr. 1 (means to correct input errors before submitting) is met by the editable form fields themselves. Nr. 3 (electronic acknowledgement of receipt without undue delay) is met on sign-up by the verification email configured at `server/accounts.ts:184-186` (`emailVerification.sendOnSignUp: true`), with the session issued straight away by Better Auth's default `autoSignIn`, noted at `server/accounts.ts:147`. Open are Nr. 2 — communicating the Art. 246c EGBGB information *in good time before* the declaration is submitted — and Nr. 4 — making the terms retrievable *at the time of conclusion* and storable in reproducible form.

**Why this is filed as P0 rather than as tidying.** Two consequences follow from the gap, both live today. First, terms that are never presented and never retrievable do not become part of the contract (§ 305 Abs. 2 BGB), so the clauses the operator is relying on do not apply: the limitation of liability (clause 11), the indemnity (5.4), the content licence (6.2) and the place of jurisdiction (13.6). The published terms say as much about themselves — clause 1.4.2 records that the application presents the conditions nowhere, and clause 2.4.4 names Nr. 2 and Nr. 4 as open. Second, § 312i is a market-conduct rule, so a breach is actionable under § 3a UWG by any competitor (§ 8 Abs. 3 Nr. 1 UWG) — a cease-and-desist letter needs no customer complaint behind it.

**The shape of the change, without choosing how to build it.** An application-wide footer carrying the three links on every route, plus one sentence with the same links at each of the three points above, naming the terms and the privacy policy before the action is taken. No mandatory checkbox is required by law; whether one is added anyway is a decision for the legal advisor and is not settled here.

**Done when.** From any route of the running application, the imprint, the privacy policy and the terms can each be reached, and each of the three contract-conclusion points named above shows a sentence that names the terms and the privacy policy and links to them, before the action is submitted. The repository's gate (`mise run check`) and its test suite, as `AGENTS.md` declares them, apply unchanged.

**A follow-up outside this repository, once this is built.** Clauses 1.4.2 and 2.4.4 of the published terms exist only to record this gap and fall away without replacement when it closes. That text is approved contract wording held in the separate private repository that owns the published legal texts, so the edit goes back to the legal advisor rather than being made here. It is named as a consequence, not taken into this requirement.

**A related weakness this requirement does not take.** The presenter-facing UI is English-only — `src/i18n.ts` is the participant-facing dictionary selected per presentation (REQ084) and covers nothing a presenter sees — while clause 1.5 of the published terms makes German the language of the contract. Art. 246c Nr. 4 EGBGB asks which languages are available for concluding the contract, and the honest answer is thinner than that clause implies. Noted here so that whoever writes the Art. 246c information has it; REQ084 is untouched and no separate requirement is opened for it yet.

**Out of scope.** Writing or amending any legal text, here or in the repository that holds them; a cookie or consent banner, which rests on a different rule; localizing the presenter UI; and any change to REQ084.
