## Description

A workspace can own decks itself rather than through one account, readable and presentable by its members and surviving any single member's removal.

## Updates

- 2026-08-14 — Workspaces own decks: workspaces/workspaceMembers collections, workspaceId on the deck (creatorId and creatorTokenHash cleared, so no account's removal takes a deck and no removed account keeps one), /api/workspaces/* plus POST /api/presentations{workspaceId} and POST /api/presentations/:id/workspace to move a deck in or out. A workspace deck is never grandfathered as pre-auth (canGrandfatherLegacyDeck) and never claimable. Client: /workspaces and /workspaces/:id pages, move-in dialog on the deck card. Covered by server/workspaces.integration.test.ts + workspaces.schemas.test.ts + src/workspaces.test.ts; docs/api.md, architecture.md and frontend.md updated.
