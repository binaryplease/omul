## Description

As a participant or presenter on a light screen, I want "Correct!", "Time's up", a "correct" label, "1 of 2" and my running score to be readable, so that the result of a quiz question is not the faintest thing on the page. This repairs REQ166 (the chrome's colour direction), whose status colours were the dark scheme's values in both schemes, and REQ056 (the verdict and the room's score line), which drew information in a tier built for captions.

- `--color-success`, `--color-warning` and `--color-error` are text colours as often as they are dots or bars, so each is held to 4.5:1 against every ground of its scheme: the chrome's Ground, Card, Raised and Hover, and the same four on every built-in deck theme. The dark values already clear it. The light scheme has its own deeper values (`#065f46`, `#8a4200`, `#a51b1b`), which the auto scheme in a dark OS takes back.
- The running total under a verdict ("Your score so far"), the answer key ("Accepted: …") and the typed question's waiting line are drawn in `text-text-muted`, not `text-text-dim`.

## Notes

Defect, **internal**: found by a UI test pass of the quiz feature on `f3bd62e` (major M6, WCAG 1.4.3). On the light ground `rgb(250,250,250)`: "Correct!", the "correct" label and "1 of 2" in `rgb(52,211,153)` measured 1.84:1. "Time's up" in `rgb(248,113,113)` measured 2.65:1. "Your score so far" in `rgb(138,138,147)` measured 3.28:1.

**Why the dim tier was not raised instead.** `--color-text-dim` is the 3:1 tier on purpose: `SLIDE_TEXT_MIN_CONTRAST.dim` is 3, every built-in deck theme carries its own value, and a deck brand derives it at weight 0.62. It is for captions and labels. Raising it would re-specify the whole ramp in both schemes and ten theme variants for three lines of information that were in the wrong tier. The dark dim (`#71717a`) measures 4.12:1 on Ground, so those lines failed in the dark scheme too.

**Measured after the fix** (Chromium, presenter 1440 × 900 and phone 390 × 844). Light: "Correct!", "correct" and "1 of 1" 7.36:1, "Time's up" 7.26:1, the score, accepted and score lines 7.89:1. Dark: 10.35:1, 7.19:1 and 7.76:1.

**Not covered.** A slide with its own canvas colour (REQ019/REQ070) gets its text ramp re-derived but not these three tokens. A status word on an authored canvas close to them can still fall short, and did before this change too.

**Guarded by** `DeckTheme.test.ts`, "the status colours read as words in both schemes (REQ222)". It parses `index.css`, measures each token on every ground of the chrome and of every built-in theme, and checks that the auto scheme's dark block restores the dark values.
