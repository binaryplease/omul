## Description

As an organizer who downloads a deck as PDF — with its results (`GET /presentations/:id/deck.pdf`) or without them (`?results=false`), the only two PDF exports the product produces — I want a document I can hand on as it is: compact, readable in my deck's terms, and showing each result in a form that says something at a glance, rather than one that reads like a dump of the export's internal fields.

Raised by a tester, verbatim: "Die PDF Exporte sind irgendwie sehr hässlich und sollten als separater Issue noch einmal überarbeitet werden und aufgehübscht werden."

## Notes

**Current behaviour.** Observed on 2026-10-23 by rendering both readings of a sample eight-slide deck (text, multiple choice, word cloud, open text, scale, ranking, quiz and leaderboard slides; six participants) through the real endpoint and rasterizing the pages with poppler. Both readings come out of `buildDeckDocument` / `renderDeckDocument` in `server/deck-pdf.ts`, so each defect below shows up in both wherever it applies:

- **One slide per page, however little it holds.** Every section starts on a fresh page (`flow.startSection()`, `server/deck-pdf.ts:1722`), and every slide is its own section (`buildDeckDocument`, `server/deck-pdf.ts:804-829`). Eight short slides take nine A4 pages, most of them three-quarters empty; the cover is a fact list across the top fifth of a page.
- **The cover prints raw internal values.** `Status`, `Mode`, `Language` and `Results visibility` print the stored enum (`live`, `live`, `en`, `instant`), and `Created at` / `Exported at` print raw ISO timestamps with milliseconds and `Z` (`2026-10-23T17:31:20.624Z`) (`coverSection`, `server/deck-pdf.ts:619-645`).
- **Every label is English, whatever the deck's language.** "Slide 2 of 8 · Multiple Choice", "Options", "Results", "Participants select one option.", the metric names and the cover labels are all fixed English strings; `presentation.language` is only printed as a cover value (`server/deck-pdf.ts:622`). A German deck reads as German questions under English chrome.
- **Results are a field list, not a chart.** Under each result bar sits a caption of every other aggregate metric (`Count: 1 · Correct: —`, `server/deck-pdf.ts:577-588`). The `Correct` metric is emitted for every multiple-choice option (`server/results-export.ts:432-458`), so a non-quiz poll prints `Correct: —` under each of its options. Shares print to two decimals (`16.67%`). The bars are 5 pt high (`server/deck-pdf.ts:1561`, `1569`). A multiple-choice or quiz slide lists its options twice: once as an "Options" bullet list and again as the bar labels right under it.
- **Some slides print a results block with nothing in it, or nothing a reader can use.** A text (content) slide prints "Results / Responses 0" (`server/deck-pdf.ts:820-822` adds results to every slide, and `tallyBlocksFor` always opens with the label, `server/deck-pdf.ts:543`). A scale slide prints `Scale minimum 1`, `Scale maximum 5`, `Skipped 0`, `Average 3.5` as label/value rows and no distribution of the answers. Metric labels read as field names: `Correct share %`, `Max points per answer`.
- **Formatting the author wrote is dropped.** Text-slide markdown is flattened to plain text (`plainTextFromMarkdown`, `server/deck-pdf.ts:204-222`), so `**Agenda**` prints as an ordinary line with no weight, the same as the body under it.

REQ158 (text outside Latin-1, closed) is not reopened by this record: which characters reach the page is settled there, and nothing here changes that coverage.

**Done when.** Both readings of a deck that has one slide of every interactive type and at least one content slide, rendered through the endpoint and rasterized, show none of the defects listed under *Current behaviour*, and the PDF tests in the gates `AGENTS.md` names stay green.

**Out of scope.**

- What each reading contains: which slides, which aggregates, the participant roster and the profanity-filtered answers stay as REQ096, REQ076 and REQ085 decided. This record is about how that content is laid out and worded, not about adding or removing data. The REQ158 font coverage and the REQ159 rendering-cost bounds hold.
- The spreadsheet export (`results.xlsx`) and the slide image export (REQ097) are not part of this record.

**Note.** The `Correct: —` caption comes from `aggregateRowsFor` in `server/results-export.ts`, which the workbook's Aggregates sheet is written from too. The workbook emits the same empty `Correct` cell for a non-quiz option; whether the sheet should keep that column is a separate question for the workbook.
