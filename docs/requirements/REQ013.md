## Description

One or more options on a multiple-choice slide can be flagged correct. The flag is withheld from non-editors until the presenter reveals it, and it produces no score on its own — scoring is the quiz slide's job (REQ056).

## Updates

- 2026-07-28 — Correct answers are no longer quiz-only. The editor's Correct toggle now shows on plain multiple-choice slides, new options carry isCorrect: false on both choice types, and the results payload emits isCorrect for a choice slide once its author marked at least one option (an explicit null when none is marked, so a slide with no notion of correctness stays distinguishable — ADR-0024; a quiz still always reports it). Every results surface — bars, donut, pie, dots — and the editor preview render the solution mark, and it appears only with the results themselves, so REQ102 reveal gating governs when the solution becomes visible. No scoring, points or leaderboard: that boundary belongs to quiz competition slides (REQ054-REQ059).
