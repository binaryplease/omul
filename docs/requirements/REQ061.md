## Description

A slide type collecting several typed fields — free text, email, choice — from one participant in a single submission, stored and exported as one row.

## Updates

- 2026-08-10 — Form slide shipped end to end: 'form' slide type in SlideTypeEnum/INTERACTIVE_SLIDE_TYPES with formFields[] (text/email/choice, optional or required, capped at 6 fields and 8 options per choice field); one participant's whole form travels as a single vote row via encodeFormSubmission/decodeFormSubmission, which enforces each field's type at the boundary (non-address, unknown option, over-long answer and blank required field are all refused) and is bounded by voteValueLimitFor, leaving every other slide type on the 500-character cap. Authored in SlideEditor with a field-type picker and a per-field required switch, drawn in the rail thumbnail, the preview pane and SlideTypeIcon/Menu; answered on the phone by ParticipantSlideView with a submit button that states why it cannot act (ADR-0025), and re-sending corrects a submission rather than adding a second. The tally reports the fill rate per field plus a choice field's per-option counts; the submitted rows are emitted only to a caller who can edit the deck and are rendered on no screen at all, since every results surface is pointed at a room — the organizer reads them in the spreadsheet export, where a whole form is one row with each answered field named by what it asked. A fresh form slide is authored resultsVisibility=private on top of that gate. Preview generates test submissions (REQ104); docs/api.md gained a Form voting and results section. Tests: server/form.schemas.test.ts, server/form.integration.test.ts, src/components/FormSlide.test.ts, plus form branches in results-export.schemas.test.ts.
