## Description

As a presenter on the presenter screen (`/present/<id>`), I want a top bar that is calm and ordered — the controls I need while running a session grouped by what they are for, with a clear visual hierarchy — instead of one long run of equal-weight chips, so that I find each action at a glance at every window width from a phone to a wide desktop.

## Notes

**Why this record exists.** REQ189 stopped the bar from scrolling sideways by splitting it into rows that wrap. That fixed the overflow and left the clutter: the owner rejected the result as looking very cluttered and needing a complete rework to be clean. REQ189 stays done; this record is the rework.

**What the bar offers today** (`src/pages/PresenterPage.tsx:856-1245`, on the branch carrying REQ189). Up to three rows inside one `<header>`:

- **Row 1** (`:864-953`): the Back link (`:865-872`, chevron, "Back" text from `sm`); the deck title (`:877`, the one element that truncates); the status badge and session clock (`:878-884`, the clock is absent until the deck has gone live); then the lifecycle group (`:891-952`). For a viewer who may edit the deck: draft → Edit (`title="Edit presentation"`) + Start ("Start Presentation" from `sm`); live → End; ended → Edit + Restart. Any other viewer sees a "View only" marker (`:892-899`) and no lifecycle button.
- **Row 2** (`:955-1233`, `flex-wrap`): join-bar toggle (`:962-978`); the inline share cluster from `2xl` only (`:985-991`); participant count, display only (`:994-997`); Q&A with open count (`:1018-1033`); audience panel with message count (`:1046-1059`); presenter notes (`:1066-1078`); slide comments with count (`:1091-1106`); roster with count (`:1121-1134`); blank screen (`:1149-1161`); the app menu, theme and legal texts (`:1164`); Preview (`:1171`, `src/components/PreviewLink.tsx:33-48`, labelled from `sm`); read-only results link (`:1198-1207`); export (`:1213-1222`); reset results (`:1223-1232`).
- **Row 3, below `2xl` only** (`:1238-1244`): the compact share cluster — join code, Link, QR, Embed, and Edit Link for a holder of the edit token (`src/components/ShareCluster.tsx:64-103`). Neither share cluster is drawn while the deck hides its join bar (REQ073).

By width: at **360px** (below `sm`) Back is a bare chevron, status and clock stack under the title, Edit and Preview are icon-only, Start reads "Start", and row 2 wraps onto more than one line. At **768px** and **1280px** the same three rows, with labels, the divider after Back, and status and clock inline beside the title; per REQ189's log row 2 holds one line at 1280px (its line count at 768px was not measured for this record). At **1536px** (`2xl`) two rows: the share cluster moves inline into row 2 right after the join-bar toggle and row 3 disappears. Draft, live and ended change only the lifecycle group and the clock; every row-2 control is present in every state, and for a viewer without edit access each one is disabled with its reason in `aria-label`/`title` (comments follow `canReadComments` rather than edit access).

**What reads as clutter today.** Row 2 is thirteen controls of the same weight in one undifferentiated sequence: live-session panels, the blank-screen switch, the app menu, Preview and the post-session actions (results link, export, reset) sit side by side with no grouping. Styles mix — Preview is a labelled `btn-secondary`, its neighbours are bordered icon chips. Two eye icons share the row (blank screen `:1160`, Preview), and two pencils share the bar (Edit in row 1, Edit Link in the share cluster). Below `2xl` the join code and its share actions sit in a third row of their own.

**Desired outcome.** The presenter top bar presents every capability it offers today in a calm, ordered layout whose grouping and visual hierarchy make each control findable at a glance, at every viewport width from 360px up.

**Done when.** In a browser at 360px, 768px, 1280px and 1536px wide, for a deck in each of the draft, live and ended states, as its editor and as a viewer without edit access, the bar follows the design recorded in these Notes, has no horizontal scroll in it or in the page, keeps the Back link, the deck title and the lifecycle button (or the View only marker) visible and clickable, and offers every capability listed above with at most one extra tap; `mise run check` and `mise run test`, as `AGENTS.md` declares them, apply unchanged.

**Constraints, stated flat.** Every capability the bar offers today stays reachable; controls may be regrouped, merged into menus, re-ordered or restyled. What each control does, its disabled-with-reason state for a viewer without edit access, and its `aria-label`/`title` stay. The Back link, the deck title and the lifecycle button (Start/End/Restart) stay visible and uncovered at every width from 360px, and nothing scrolls horizontally. The design itself is left open here; the UI/UX concept stage records it in these Notes before the build.

**Out of scope.** The editor top bar (`src/pages/CreatePage.tsx`), the participant screen, and the slide rail below the bar (`src/pages/PresenterPage.tsx:1289`) are not part of this requirement. The panels, overlays and dialogs the controls open (Q&A, audience, notes, comments, roster, QR, results link, export, reset confirmation) keep their contents; only how the bar reaches them is in question.

**Note.** `src/pages/PresenterPage.test.ts` pins REQ189's structure — lifecycle in the first row, every other control in the wrapping row — so a layout that moves controls into menus will contradict those assertions, which then describe REQ189's layout rather than this one. The comments inside the bar (`:856-861`, `:980-984`, `:1185-1191`) likewise justify the REQ189 layout.

**Design (UI/UX concept stage, 2026-10-11) — build exactly this.** It was drawn as an interactive concept at 360, 768, 1280 and 1536px wide, for draft, live and ended decks, as editor and as view-only, and measured there. Nothing below is an option. Every line numbered `:NNN` refers to `src/pages/PresenterPage.tsx` as cited above.

*The idea.* The bar has three zones, sorted by what each control is for, and exactly one primary button. **Identity** is which deck this is and how it is doing. **Room console** is how people get in, what the projector shows, and the panels the presenter reads. **Command** is running the deck. Reading matter (status, clock, head-count) stops looking like buttons. Everything about the deck that is not the lifecycle moves into one **Deck** menu. The third row is gone. From 1280px the whole bar is one line.

*Layout by width.* The `<header>` keeps its shell (`border-b border-border bg-surface/80 backdrop-blur-sm`). Its inner element becomes a grid: `grid grid-cols-[minmax(0,1fr)_auto] xl:grid-cols-[minmax(0,1fr)_auto_auto] items-center gap-x-2 sm:gap-x-3 xl:gap-x-6 gap-y-2 px-4 sm:px-6 py-2`. Its three children, in this DOM order (which is also the tab order), are:

- Identity: `col-start-1 row-start-1 flex items-center gap-1.5 sm:gap-3 min-w-0`
- Room console: `col-span-2 row-start-2 xl:col-span-1 xl:col-start-2 xl:row-start-1 flex items-center gap-2 sm:gap-3 min-w-0`
- Command: `col-start-2 row-start-1 xl:col-start-3 flex items-center gap-1.5 sm:gap-2`

| Viewport | Lines | Line 1 | Line 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| below `sm` (360px) | 2 | Back as a bare chevron · title over status line · Deck (icon) · lifecycle (short label) · app menu | Door (code without the "Join:" label) · Screen · Panels as one menu button |
| `sm` to below `xl` (640–1279px, so 768px) | 2 | Back with label · divider · title over status line · Deck (labelled) · lifecycle · app menu | Door · Screen · Panels inline |
| `xl` and up (1280px, 1536px) | 1 | Identity · Room console · Command | — |

No row wraps, and nothing uses `flex-wrap` except the phone status line (below). Measured in the concept: at 360px the console takes about 282 of 328px. At 1280px the identity zone keeps at least 366px in the widest case (a draft deck, "Start Presentation"), which fits a 40-character title untruncated. At 1536px it keeps about 620px. Below `xl` the console sits visually under the command zone while coming before it in tab order; that is accepted.

*Identity zone.*

- **Back**: the existing button (`ChevronLeft` 16, "Back" from `sm`), plus `min-h-9`.
- **Divider**: `w-px h-7 bg-border hidden sm:block flex-shrink-0`.
- **Title block**: `flex flex-col min-w-0`. The `<h1>` keeps `font-semibold truncate min-w-0` and gains `title={pres.title}`, so a truncated title can still be read. It stays the only element in the bar that gives way.
- **Status line**: directly under the title at every width: `flex sm:flex-nowrap max-sm:flex-wrap items-center gap-x-1.5 sm:gap-x-2 text-xs text-text-muted`. It holds, in order:
  1. `<StatusBadge/>`, unchanged.
  2. `<SessionClock/>`, unchanged. Still absent on a draft.
  3. The participant count, moved here out of the chip row. It is display-only, so it gets no border and no button chrome: `<span className="inline-flex items-center gap-1 font-mono tabular-nums flex-shrink-0" title="Participants in the room"><Users size={14} aria-hidden /><span className="sr-only">Participants: </span>{participantCount}</span>`.
- **Separators**: from `sm`, a 3px dot sits between neighbouring items: `<span aria-hidden className="hidden sm:block size-[3px] rounded-full bg-text-dim" />`.
- **Phone fallback**: below `sm` there are no dots, and the line may wrap to a second line. This happens for an ended deck (Restart is wider), for a view-only viewer, and for a session past an hour. That wrap is the intended fallback. A live or draft deck fits on one line.
- Do not use `whitespace-nowrap`. Use `flex-shrink-0` on the items.

*Shared tokens for the console.* Export these beside `ICON_BUTTON_HOVER` in `src/components/ShareCluster.tsx` (the token's home, which the hover guard allowlists) and document them in `docs/frontend.md`:

- `BAR_GROUP = "flex flex-none items-center gap-0.5 rounded-lg border border-border bg-surface-raised p-0.5"`: one pill per group, with no borders on the items inside.
- `BAR_GROUP_ITEM`: the composition of `ICON_BUTTON_HOVER` with `flex h-8 min-w-8 items-center justify-center gap-1.5 rounded-md px-2 text-sm hover:bg-surface-hover aria-pressed:bg-accent-dim aria-pressed:text-accent-text aria-expanded:bg-accent-dim aria-expanded:text-accent-text disabled:opacity-50 disabled:cursor-not-allowed disabled:hover:bg-transparent disabled:hover:text-text-muted`. Every item is 32px square at minimum.
- **Pressed state**: a toggle's pressed state is this fill, which replaces today's `border-accent`. The fill is a luminance change, not a hue change alone. For the join-bar and blank toggles the icon also changes.
- **Counts**: `font-mono text-xs tabular-nums`. Use `text-text` for a positive number and `text-text-dim` for `0` and `—`. A pressed item's count inherits the accent text.

*Room console: three groups, always in this order.*

1. **Door**. Drawn only while `joinBarShown`, exactly as today (REQ073). It is one `BAR_GROUP`, rendered by a new `<ShareCluster variant="menu">`. This variant replaces both `bar` and `compact`; delete those two, since nothing else composes them. `buildShareControls()` stays the single descriptor, unchanged. The group holds:
   - **The join-code button**. It still copies the code on one tap, with `title="Copy join code"` and the `Copy`/`Check` 14 icon. "Join:" is `text-xs text-text-muted` from `sm` and `sr-only` below. The code is `font-mono font-bold text-accent-text tracking-widest text-lg` from `sm`, and `text-base tracking-wider` below.
   - A `w-px h-5 bg-border` divider.
   - **The Share trigger**: a `BAR_GROUP_ITEM` holding `ChevronDown` 14, with `title="Share"`. Its `aria-label` is "Share — copy the join link, show the QR code, copy the embed code", plus ", or copy the edit link" when the descriptor carries that action.

   The trigger opens the **Share menu**: one row per descriptor action, in descriptor order. The edit-link row sits after a divider. Each row:
   - Shows the action's icon and its `title` as visible text ("Copy join link", "Show QR code", "Copy embed code (iframe snippet)", "Copy share link with edit permissions"), and keeps that `title`.
   - A copy row swaps to `Check` + `copiedLabel` for the existing confirmation window, and the menu stays open so the confirmation is seen.
   - QR closes the menu, then opens the existing overlay. The edit link closes the menu, then opens the existing confirmation.
2. **Screen**. Always drawn: one `BAR_GROUP` with `role="group" aria-label="Shared screen"`. These are the two switches that take something off the projector, so they sit side by side.
   - **The join-bar toggle**: today's handler, `aria-pressed={!joinBarShown}`, label and icons (`PanelTop`/`PanelTopDashed`). It moves from first in the old row to here.
   - **The blank toggle**: today's handler, `aria-pressed` and label. The icon becomes `Monitor` when showing and `MonitorOff` when blanked, which ends the clash with Preview's eye. Both are `BAR_GROUP_ITEM`.
3. **Panels**. `role="group" aria-label="Panels"`.
   - **From `sm`**: one `BAR_GROUP` holding five `BAR_GROUP_ITEM`s, in this order: Q&A (`MessageCircleQuestion` + open count or `—`), Audience (`MessagesSquare` + message count), Notes (`StickyNote`, no count), Comments (`MessageSquare` + count or `—`), Who took part (`UserRound` + roster count or `—`). Each keeps today's handler, `disabled` (comments still follow `canReadComments`), `aria-pressed`, `aria-label` and `title`. The panels stay independent, and several may be open at once.
   - **Below `sm`**: the group holds a single trigger `BAR_GROUP_ITEM`. It shows `PanelRight` 16, then the number of open panels in the count style (only while at least one is open), then `ChevronDown` 14. Its `aria-label` is "Panels — Q&A, audience, notes, comments, who took part", with `title="Panels"`. It opens the **Panels menu**: the same five controls as rows, in the same order and with the same semantics. The visible text is "Q&A", "Audience", "Presenter notes", "Comments", "Who took part". The count sits right-aligned, and a `Check` appears when a row is pressed. Toggling a row leaves the menu open.

*Command zone, in this order.*

1. **Deck trigger**. From `sm` it is `flex h-9 items-center gap-1.5 rounded-lg border border-border bg-surface-raised px-3 text-sm font-medium text-text hover:bg-surface-hover aria-expanded:border-accent aria-expanded:bg-accent-dim aria-expanded:text-accent-text`. It shows `GalleryHorizontalEnd` 16, then "Deck", then `ChevronDown` 14 in `text-text-dim`. Below `sm` it is icon-only (`w-9 justify-center px-0`) with "Deck" `sr-only`. Its `aria-label` is "Deck — preview, share results, export, reset", with "edit, " after the dash when the Edit row is present. The name starts with the visible word.

   The trigger opens the **Deck menu**, top to bottom:
   - **Edit presentation** (`Pencil`): for the same viewers and states as today's Edit button, so an editor of a draft or ended deck. It navigates to the editor. A divider follows.
   - **Preview** (`Eye`): rendered by `PreviewLink` through a new `variant="menu-row"`. That variant always shows the label and keeps the component's own `title` and disabled logic. The editor's use of `PreviewLink` is unchanged. A divider follows.
   - **Share results…** (`Link2`): opens `ResultsLinkDialog`.
   - **Export…** (`Download`): opens `ExportDialog`. A divider follows.
   - **Reset results…** (`RefreshCw`): `text-error` while enabled, and opens the existing confirmation.

   Each row keeps today's `aria-label`/`title` string exactly (`resultsLinkButtonLabel`, `exportControlLabel`, `resetButtonLabel`, PreviewLink's title). Each is disabled for a viewer without edit access, as today. A disabled row also shows its reason as a visible second line in `text-xs text-text-dim`: the label's text after its first " — ", with the first letter capitalised, or PreviewLink's whole title. Activating a row closes the menu first. The second pencil and the labelled Preview leave the bar this way.
2. **Lifecycle**. Today's buttons and handlers, minus Edit:
   - draft: Start, "Start Presentation" from `sm`, "Start" below
   - live: End
   - ended: Restart
   - a viewer without edit access: the View only marker, unchanged

   Each button is 36px tall, with 12px horizontal padding below `sm` and 16px from `sm`. `.btn-primary`/`.btn-secondary` in `src/index.css` are unlayered and beat utilities, so set this with Tailwind's important suffix (`h-9 py-0! px-3! sm:px-4!`). End keeps its error outline.
3. **App menu**. `<AppMenu/>`, unchanged, last in the bar.

*Menus.* The Share, Panels and Deck menus are one new shared primitive, `src/components/ui/BarMenu.tsx`. It factors the trigger and portaled panel out of `AppMenu`:

- **Same mechanics as AppMenu**: a disclosure, not `role="menu"`. The trigger has `aria-expanded`. The panel is portaled to the nearest theme scope, placed by `appMenuPlacement()`, and closes on Escape, an outside click or tabbing off either end. Focus returns to the trigger.
- **On open**: focus goes to the first enabled row. If no row is enabled, it goes to the panel itself (`tabIndex={-1}`).
- **AppMenu**: it composes the same shell, and its behaviour, name and exports do not change.
- **Panel look**: as AppMenu's: `w-64 max-w-[calc(100vw-1rem)] rounded-xl border border-border bg-surface p-1.5 shadow-2xl popover-in`, with an `xs` uppercase heading ("Share · join <code>", "Deck", "Panels").
- **Rows**: AppMenu's `MENU_ROW` treatment, with `items-start` so a second line fits. Disabled rows use `opacity-50` on the icon and label only, never on the reason line.
- **Dividers**: AppMenu's `SECTION_DIVIDER`.
- **Docs**: record `BarMenu` in `docs/frontend.md`.

*What a view-only viewer sees.*

- The command zone is Deck, View only and the app menu.
- The Deck menu has no Edit row, and its four rows are all disabled with their visible reasons.
- The Share menu lacks the edit link, because the descriptor already omits it.
- The Screen toggles and four of the panels are disabled with their reasons. Comments follows `canReadComments`.
- Q&A and Who took part read `—`.

*What the deck's state changes.* Draft, live and ended change only the lifecycle button, the Edit row, the clock and the counts. The layout never moves.
