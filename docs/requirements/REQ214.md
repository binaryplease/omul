## Description

As an organizer running a scored quiz, I want the shared screen to keep the answer to a quiz question off the projector while the room is still answering it, so that nobody can read the solution, or copy the majority, off the wall and the score means something. This repairs REQ056 (the answer key is withheld until the question is over) and REQ015 (an `instant` slide's results), which disagreed on the one surface the whole room reads.

While a quiz question is running, the shared screen shows the question, the countdown and the response count, and nothing else: no correct mark, no "Accepted:" list, no per-option bars, no typed answers and no score line. When the question is over — the window closed with its submission grace, the presenter closed it to answers, its results were revealed, or the deck ended — the slide's reveal setting decides as it does everywhere: `instant` shows the results at that moment by itself, with no new answer needed to re-render the screen; `on-click` waits for the presenter's reveal; `private` never shows them. A plain multiple-choice slide with a marked option is not a quiz and keeps showing its tally and solution live (REQ013).

## Notes

Defect, **internal**: found by a UI test pass of the quiz feature on `f3bd62e` (blocker B1), not by a business need. Reproduced on `f48fbe0` before the fix.

**What was wrong.** The presenter page `/present/<id>` is what gets projected, and it is held by an editor. The payload gate (`solutionVisibleToAudience`, REQ056) lets an editor read a running question's answer key, and `tallyVisibleToAudience` publishes an `instant` slide's tally as each answer lands. Nothing else stood between the two on the shared screen. So with the default reveal setting (the "Quiz round" template uses it), a select question showed the green "correct" mark and the live per-option bars, and a typed question showed "Accepted: …" and the typed answers marked correct, all with the clock still running. `readChoiceTally` in `src/components/Results.tsx` even fell back to the authored `isCorrect` on purpose, to keep the mark steady between the presenter's authenticated poll and the withheld broadcast.

**What closes it.**

- `quizQuestionIsRunning(slide, deck, now)` in `server/schemas.ts` is the one reading of "the room is still answering this": a quiz whose solution the audience may not have yet (`solutionVisibleToAudience`) and which is still open to submissions (`slideAcceptsSubmissions`, REQ111). An untimed question, and one not yet opened, run until they are closed, revealed or the deck ends.
- `useQuizQuestionRunning` in `src/components/QuizTimer.tsx` reads it on the server's clock and schedules one re-render for the instant the grace runs out. The countdown alone could not be the trigger, because it stops at the deadline, before the grace ends.
- `PresenterSlideView` (the live shared screen and the preview's presenter pane) draws "Results appear when the time is up." (or, untimed, "…once the question is closed to answers.") instead of the tally while the question runs. The `private` and `on-click` gates are unchanged and checked first.
- `PresenterPage` restarts its results poll the moment the question ends, so the editor's full tally replaces a withheld broadcast at once rather than up to three seconds later.
- The shared results page (`SharedResultsPage`, REQ098) applies the same gate: it holds the per-option counts for a link holder or the owner, and it is one tab away from a participant's phone.
- The editor canvas's sample results (REQ154) are authoring, not the shared screen, and keep their marks. Participant phones already waited for the question to close (`quizResultReady`) and are not changed here.

**What this does not cover.** The wire. Under `instant`, the public results endpoint and the `results.updated` broadcast still carry a running select question's per-option counts to everyone (the answer key and the typed answers are already withheld there). No screen draws them any more, but a client that reads its own socket can. That is tracked as REQ215.

**How it is held.** `server/quiz.schemas.test.ts` covers `quizQuestionIsRunning`: inside the window and its grace, past the grace, closed to answers, revealed, ended, untimed, not opened, and a plain choice slide. `src/components/PresenterSlideView.test.ts` renders the shared screen to static markup. While a select or typed question runs there is no correct mark, no option text, no "Accepted:" and no score line. Once it is over, `instant` shows them, closing submissions shows them with time left, `private` and `on-click` still hold them, and a plain choice slide shows its solution live. The three running-question cases fail against the old `PresenterSlideView`.
